# ComputAI

**Where your compute and AI money goes.**

Your Claude and Codex subscriptions, local models, homelab GPUs and cloud GPU rentals — in one ledger of
tokens, GPU-hours, kWh and money. One file, standard-library Python, nothing to install on your machines.

一眼看完你的算力和 AI 花費：Claude、Codex、本地模型、homelab、雲端 GPU，放在同一本帳裡。

> Work in progress. See [GOAL.md](GOAL.md) for the plan. A sibling of [slurmtop](https://github.com/Sean-Hawks/slurmtop).
